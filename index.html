Help

Getting started

  1. Choose the camera and the sound, click Start.
  2. Choose the screen, click Open projection window, then click once inside that window to make it full screen.
  3. Choose a mode.

With the ATEM
Choose the ATEM as Camera and as Sound. The camera's sound goes into the ATEM through HDMI and reaches the computer through the same USB cable. In the ATEM, the camera's sound must be switched on.

Modes
Fixed delay: everything is shown again after a set time, continuously.
Sentence by sentence: the person speaks; when they stop, their sentence is played back once, picture and sound. What is said during the playback is not recorded.

Play now
Ends the sentence being recorded and plays it back right away. If nothing is being recorded, plays the last sentence again.

Settings
Level: the bar moves with the sound. The red line is the speech level; above it, the bar turns black and it counts as speech.
Speech level: set it just above the noise of the room.
Silence to end: how long the person must be silent before the sentence is played back.
Lip sync: only if voice and lips do not match; move until they do.
Shortest speech: shorter sounds (a cough, a click) are ignored.
Extra time before / after: keeps a moment before the first word and after the last one.
Pause after playback: waiting time before listening again.
Longest sentence: past this, the sentence is played back anyway.

Transitions (sentence by sentence)
Dissolve in: the waiting image melts into the new echo. Keep it shorter than Extra time before so the first word is fully visible.
Slow down at end: the echo slows down to a stop instead of freezing abruptly. The picture ends a little after the sound.
Waiting image: how the last picture looks while the person speaks. Strength is how strong the effect gets, over is how long it takes.
Trails: movements leave a fading trace. Amount is how long the trace stays.
